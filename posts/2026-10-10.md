# 2026-10-10 — Day 12: Text Case Converter

Shipped `day-12-text-case-converter`: a stdlib-only CLI that converts text between 12 cases (upper, lower, title, sentence, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE and more). It splits words smartly, even from camelCase, and works on inline text, files, or piped input, with an `--all` mode to see every style at once.
