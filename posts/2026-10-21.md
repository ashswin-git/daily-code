# 2026-10-21 — Day 23: IP Address Checker

Shipped `day-23-ip-address-checker`: a stdlib-only CLI that validates IPv4/IPv6 addresses and CIDR ranges, labels them (public, private, loopback, CGNAT, documentation...), shows binary/integer/reverse-pointer forms, works as a quick subnet calculator, and can add free geo/ISP info or show your own public IP.
