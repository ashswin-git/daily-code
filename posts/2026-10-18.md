# 2026-10-18 — Day 20: Fake Data Generator

Shipped `day-20-fake-data-generator`: a stdlib-only CLI that generates realistic fake records for testing and demos, with 5 presets (person, user, employee, address, order), 28 mixable fields, matching name/username/email per record, seeded reproducible output, and table, JSON, JSONL or CSV export to a file.
