# 2026-10-19 — Day 21: Email Validator

Shipped `day-21-email-validator`: a stdlib-only CLI that checks email addresses with clear syntax error reasons, typo hints for popular domains (gmial.com → gmail.com), disposable and role-inbox flags, an optional `--dns` domain check, and text, JSON or CSV output plus a `--valid-only` mode for cleaning mailing lists.
