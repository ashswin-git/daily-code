# 2026-10-20 — Day 22: Phone Number Validator

Shipped `day-22-phone-number-validator`: a stdlib-only, offline CLI that cleans messy phone numbers, detects the country from the calling code (18 countries), checks length and mobile prefixes, and prints E.164 plus a friendly national format, with `--country` for local numbers, text/JSON/CSV output and a `--valid-only` mode for cleaning contact lists.
