# 2026-10-31 — Day 33: TXT to JSON Converter

Shipped `day-33-txt-to-json-converter`: a stdlib-only CLI that turns plain text into JSON. Lines become a list, `key: value` notes become an object, delimited text becomes rows, and paragraphs become a list, with auto-detection, optional type conversion (numbers, booleans, null), stdin support and file output.
